Topics

  • Classical Electrodynamics:
    1. Maxwell’s equations in covariant form, Electromagnetic field tensor, Lorentz transformation law for the electromagnetic fields and the fields due to a point charge in uniform motion; Field invariants; Covariance of Lorentz force equation and the equation of motion of a charged particle in an electromagnetic field; The generalised momentum; Energymomentum tensor and the conservation laws for the electromagnetic field; Relativistic Lagrangian and Hamiltonian of a charged particle in an electromagnetic field.
